\textbf{Uncalibrated Speed Estimation} & Fixed $0.05\,\text{m/px}$ ratio lacks perspective homography calibration matrix. & Speed values are relative visual motion estimates; background speeds are underestimated. & Calibrate camera with 4-point ground-plane planar homography transform matrix $H \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$. \\

\textbf{Field-of-View Queue Delay} & Monocular camera cannot observe queues extending beyond optical lens horizon. & Estimated clearance delay is reported as \texttt{ESTIMATE\_UNAVAILABLE}. & Deploy networked multi-camera setups along upstream approaches or ingest municipal radar sensors. \\
\textbf{Geodesic vs. Road Distance} & Haversine calculates great-circle spherical distance (aerial path). & Distance is $20\%\text{--}40\%$ shorter than actual road topology with flyover ramps. & Integrate open-source road network routing engines (OSRM / OpenStreetMap graph routing). \\

\section{Proposed Future Technical Roadmap}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Camera Matrix Calibration Module}: Implementation of an interactive calibration tool allowing traffic operators to click four physical ground lane markers with known surveyed distances, generating a perspective rectification matrix.
    \item \textbf{Automatic License Plate Recognition (ALPR)}: Integration of secondary optical character recognition (OCR) models (e.g., PaddleOCR) for automated challan generation on confirmed red-light jumps.
    \item \textbf{Arterial Green-Wave Coordination}: Expanding the single-junction proportional algorithm to support coordinated phase offsets across adjacent intersections along Outer Ring Road and Hosur Road.
\end{enumerate}
